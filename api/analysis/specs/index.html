striqt.analysis.specs¶

Spec structs for captures, measurement parameters, and analysis groups, which also define the YAML, JSON, and zarr schema.

Analysis ¶

Bases: SpecBase

the base of every measurement spec.

The fields of a subclass are the keyword arguments of one measurement.

Bases: Capture

the projection of a capture onto the fields that a measurement may read.

Two captures with equal values in these fields project to equal instances.

AnalysisFilter ¶

Bases: SpecBase

the overlap-add filter and resampler design applied to the IQ of a capture.

nfft is the block size (in samples) of the filter and nfft_out the block size of its output, so a nfft_out other than nfft resamples by nfft_out / nfft and None selects nfft. window and nfft set the transition band of the filter.

BuiltinAnalyses ¶

Bases: AnalysisGroup

the analysis: block of a sweep: one optional field per registered measurement.

A field set to a spec enables the measurement with those parameters, and None leaves the measurement out. to_dict omits the None fields.

BuiltinTriggers ¶

Bases: AnalysisGroup

the struct form of the signal_trigger: field of a source.

One optional field per trigger, and exactly one is set. The set field names the trigger, and the value is the spec of the trigger measurement. The analysis: block need not enable that measurement.

Cellular5GNPSSSync ¶

Bases: _Cellular5GNRSSBSync

the keyword block of the cellular_5g_pss_sync measurement and signal trigger.

The correlator fields are those of Cellular5GNRPSSCorrelator without max_block_count, and the correlation includes every block in the capture. The peak search averages the correlation power over the first max_beams candidate blocks of the burst set, or every candidate block when None, and over the ports when per_port is False, and weights the lags of each symbol by a window that spans window_fill of one symbol period.

Cellular5GNRPSSCorrelator ¶

Bases: _Cellular5GNRSSBCorrelator

the keyword block of cellular_5g_pss_correlation.

The band at frequency_offset is shifted to baseband and resampled to sample_rate, and delay * sample_rate must be a whole number. With symbol_indexes set to 'auto', the SSB case (3GPP TS 38.213 §4.1) is determined by subcarrier_spacing, shared_spectrum, and the capture center frequency, and a max_lag_symbols of None selects a lag span of 4 symbols, or 5 when the candidate blocks of the case are 5 or more symbols apart. The output holds one block per discovery_periodicity in the capture up to max_block_count, or every block when that count is None.

CellularCyclicAutocorrelator ¶

Bases: Analysis

the keyword block of cellular_cyclic_autocorrelation.

frame_range and symbol_range are a count of indexes from 0 or a (start, stop) pair as for the Python builtin range, and must select at least one 10 ms frame inside the capture and at least one symbol in [-14, 13]. generation and subcarrier_spacings select the OFDM numerologies correlated, one symbol lag per spacing, and frame_slots must hold a downlink slot at each spacing, where None fills the frame with downlink.

CellularResourcePowerHistogram ¶

Bases: Analysis

the keyword block of cellular_resource_power_histogram.

subcarrier_spacing and cyclic_prefix set the resource grid of the STFT: one bin per subcarrier, or per 6 or 12 subcarriers when average_rbs is 'half' or True, and one hop of window per OFDM symbol. The symbols are split into downlink and uplink by frame_slots, where None fills the frame with downlink and an 's' slot requires special_symbols. With average_slots, the power is averaged over each slot, which must span a whole number of hops, and power_low, power_high, and power_resolution set the bin edges of the histogram of the grid elements outside the guard_bandwidths.

CyclicChannelPower ¶

Bases: Analysis

the keyword block of cyclic_channel_power.

detector_period must be a whole number of samples at the capture sample_rate, cyclic_period a whole number of detector periods, and the capture duration a whole number of cycles. FrequencyAnalysisSpecBase ¶

Bases: Analysis

the STFT settings shared by the spectrum measurements.

The FFT size is the capture sample_rate divided by frequency_resolution, and the quotient must be a whole number. fractional_overlap is the overlap of consecutive windows and window_fill the nonzero part of each window, both as fractions of that size. integration_bandwidth is the width of the adjacent bins summed after the FFT, a multiple of frequency_resolution, or None for no summing. trim_stopband True limits the frequency axis to the analysis_bandwidth of the capture when that bandwidth is finite.

IQWaveform ¶

Bases: Analysis

the keyword block of iq_waveform.

The returned samples run from start_time_sec to stop_time_sec, in s from the start of the capture and truncated to a whole sample at the capture sample_rate; None selects the start or the end of the capture, and a bound outside the capture is clamped to the capture.

PowerSpectralDensity ¶

Bases: FrequencyAnalysisSpecBase

the keyword block of power_spectral_density.

The STFT fields are those of FrequencyAnalysisSpecBase. power_spectral_density reduces the spectrogram of those fields over time once per entry of time_statistic, so the output holds one trace per entry. 'mean' is the Welch estimate, and a float in [0, 1] a quantile.

Tolerance ¶

Bases: SpecBase

the roundoff error budget of one measurement output.

The budget depends on the capture and the spec alone, not on the output values. Elements more than off_peak_dBc.peak below the peak are not bounded. The peak is the matched-filter bin of the matched input, e.g., the FFT bin at the frequency of a tone or the detector sample at the time of an impulse.
